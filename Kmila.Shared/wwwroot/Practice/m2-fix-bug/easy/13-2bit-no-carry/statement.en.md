## 2-bit adder — find and fix the bug

This circuit should add two 2-bit numbers `a` and `b` plus a
carry-in `cin`, producing a 2-bit `sum` and a final `cout`. The
author wrote each output bit as an independent half-adder and
**forgot to propagate the carry** from bit 0 to bit 1.

Find the bug and connect the carries properly.

### Specification

* `{cout, sum} = a + b + cin` (3-bit unsigned result).

### Hint

Bit 1 of the sum has to take into account a possible carry
generated by bit 0.
