**Your goal:** drag steps from the toolbox on the left and stack them into a working pizza recipe. Press **Run** to watch the kitchen execute your recipe sequentially — one step at a time, top to bottom.

**Hint — try this order for the first run:**

1. Knead the dough
2. Spread the sauce
3. Add the cheese
4. Bake at 220°C
5. Slice and serve

> Notice that the order matters. Try reordering the steps and pressing Run again — the player only knows a small catalog of pre-baked recipes for now, so unusual orders may show a friendly "I don't know how to run this combination yet" hint.

This is what life inside a VHDL `process` looks like: every statement waits for the one above to finish before starting.
