**Your goal:** give the four cooks their tasks — chop, marinate, fry and boil — and press **Run**.

Every cook has their own station, so all the tasks start **at the same instant** and the kitchen finishes when the longest one does. This is **real parallelism**: what hardware does. The VHDL simulator only *imitates* it with one waitress and an event queue — see the lesson *Why VHDL can't do true parallel*.
